# Community Code of Conduct

AI Project Kit Factory welcomes constructive contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and patient. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Give useful, specific feedback; credit others' work; honor privacy, accessibility and intellectual-property boundaries. Disagree without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal insults, repeated unwanted contact, doxxing, sexualized abuse, deliberate disclosure of secrets or confidential data, and abuse of issue/PR spaces are not acceptable.

## Scope

These standards apply to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other official project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or close discussions when necessary. Enforcement should be proportionate, evidence-based and consistent.

## Reporting and review

Do not publish personal or sensitive allegations in a public issue. Ask the repository maintainers for a confidential contact route without including sensitive details; use an established private channel when one exists. If the report concerns a software vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. A maintainer who is implicated should not solely adjudicate their own report. We will handle reports discreetly to the extent feasible, avoid retaliation, and tell the reporter what follow-up is possible.

## Attribution

This is a project-specific policy drafted for AI Project Kit Factory, not a representation that another organization's code-of-conduct template has been adopted.
